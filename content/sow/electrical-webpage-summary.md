## Virtual Electrical Wiring — Proof of Concept

### A focused 3-week technical demonstration

Superimmersive proposes a focused **3-week Proof of Concept** demonstrating an interactive **basic lighting circuit** in VR on **Meta Quest 3**.

The POC will allow a learner to:

* Enter a virtual electrical training environment.
* Identify the components required to complete a basic lighting circuit.
* Connect the relevant terminals between a **distribution board, light switch and light fitting**.
* Create and position virtual wires between connection points.
* Receive clear feedback when an incorrect connection is attempted.
* Complete the circuit and activate the virtual light.
* Operate the light switch and observe the result.
* Reset the exercise and repeat the wiring task.

### The POC

**3 Weeks**
Development and refinement of the core virtual wiring and lighting-circuit experience.

**Meta Quest 3**
Target VR platform with hand and/or controller interaction.

**R85,000**
Fixed POC development fee.

The POC is intentionally limited to a **single basic lighting circuit and defined set of electrical components**. It is intended to demonstrate the technical feasibility and educational potential of interactive electrical training in VR, rather than deliver a complete electrical installation or training curriculum.

### Potential Next Step

Following evaluation, the POC could be expanded into a more comprehensive electrical training solution incorporating:

* **Additional Circuit Types** — More complex lighting and electrical circuits.
* **Unguided Wiring** — Learners complete circuits independently without visual guides.
* **Circuit Diagrams** — Learners interpret a circuit diagram and reproduce the circuit in VR.
* **Fault Finding** — Learners diagnose incorrect connections and simulated electrical faults.
* **Electrical Testing** — Virtual use of instruments such as multimeters to test circuits.
* **Electrical Safety** — Safe isolation, hazard identification and electrical safety procedures.
* **Advanced Assessment** — Tracking wiring accuracy, sequence, errors and successful circuit operation.

**Proof of Concept → Evaluation → Expanded Training Module**
